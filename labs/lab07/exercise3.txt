Checkoff Question 1:
The blocked transpose is faster than naive transpose starting at n=100 on this machine. The performance advantage becomes much more significant as matrix size increases beyond CPU cache capacity.

Checkoff Question 2:
When the matrix is very small, the entire matrix fits into CPU cache. The naive implementation already has few cache misses, and the extra loop overhead from blocking limits gains. 
Once the matrix exceeds cache size, naive transpose suffers heavy cache misses caused by strided column access on dst. Blocking operates on small submatrices that fit into cache, improves memory locality and drastically reduces cache misses, resulting in large speedups.
